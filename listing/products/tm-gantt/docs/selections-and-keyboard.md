# Navigate and select

Use **Zoom in**, **Zoom out**, **Fit to data**, and **Jump to today** to move along the timeline. **Select Activities in view** selects loaded Activities that overlap the visible time range. You can also zoom with the mouse wheel or supported touch gestures.

In analysis mode, select an Activity or drag a rectangle across the Plot area to enter Qlik's modal selection flow. Hold **Shift** while dragging across the Timeline header to select Activities in a time span. Use the vertical scrollbar to move through rows.

Keyboard focus moves through Group rows, Activity rows, bars, and Overflow contents. Press **Enter** on a Group row to select its dimension value. Use its Expand or Collapse button to change the group state with **Enter** or **Space**. The Pane splitter can be focused and resized with Arrow Left or Arrow Right; hold **Shift** for a larger step. Press **Escape** to close a transient control or undo the current run of splitter resizes.
