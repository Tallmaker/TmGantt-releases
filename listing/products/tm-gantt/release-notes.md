## New

- A native Qlik **Styling** tab follows Appearance. Qlik draws the title, subtitle, footnote, border, and shadow.
- The chart surface follows the General background color. Transparent colors blend with the theme background. Chart text keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast, using black or white when needed.

## Changed

None in this release.

## Fixed

None in this release.

## Before you upgrade

- A saved chart without a Styling value looks the same.
- Activity, progress, milestone, summary, and Row decoration colors do not change with the background.
- A background image shows outside the chart surface.
- Qlik does not document the Styling panel, and a Qlik Cloud release can change it.
